# Paddle interpretation — ITER-001

Local X=(1,0,0), R(a)=(0,-sin(a),cos(a)), T(a)=(0,-cos(a),-sin(a)). Plane of wheel: span(Y,Z), normal X. A paddle whose plane includes X is perpendicular to the wheel plane for **any** radial/tangential pitch.

V2: plane span(X,T); normal R. At the bottom of an undershot wheel this is nearly horizontal and presents little board area to transverse stream velocity. V3 leading candidate: plane span(X,R); normal T. At the bottom this gives a transverse, stream-facing board. Both satisfy n·X=0. Therefore the exact mismatch is **local radial/tangential pitch**, not failure of the original mathematical wheel-plane perpendicularity.

Source PHOTO-6854 and V2-IMG_6809 show outward-projecting boards beside overlapping vessels. They support trying the radial candidate. Perspective does not establish pitch in degrees. The existing scan is incomplete and unregistered; no robust labeled blade plane is claimed. Thorsten's correction is preserved as an expert constraint, while 0° radial pitch, 15° pitched alternative and 90° V2 comparison remain controllable.

Axial overhang and phase are separate parameters. Collision trials selected 70 mm overhang and 0.45 slot phase; these values are not source measurements. Side A/B-to-land/water and operating direction still require human field confirmation.
