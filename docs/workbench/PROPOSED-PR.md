# Field reconstruction workbench alpha: model, evidence and local capture

The research page presents findings but cannot yet support a person selecting a part, answering a concrete field question and preserving evidence before disassembly. This change adds a separate `/werkstatt/` workflow while keeping the landing page.

- Shared Three.js hypothesis geometry and five A3 review drawings, with 3 continuous arms per plane, segmented rings, expected vessel/paddle slots, stationary placeholders and sourced 24/22 mm A/B variants.
- Component/claim/evidence/conflict/task inspector; explicit orbit, fixed views, reset, pick, layers, explode, clipping and an unregistered GLB experiment.
- Measurement, observation, identification, expert narrative and risk records, with review, draft resume, photo bytes, IndexedDB and lossless JSON export/import.
- Original evidence and baseline contradictions are unchanged. Historical slots are not physical IDs; unknown current dimensions stay null.

Validation: model/record unit tests, Astro check/build and evidence verification pass locally. Shared-mesh SVG/PDF review sheets are committed. Browser acceptance is pending because local preview/browser infrastructure failed. A CI job supplies 320 px, iPhone touch-profile, desktop, 200 % text, targets and capture/resume/export checks; results must be retrieved before claiming alpha ready.
