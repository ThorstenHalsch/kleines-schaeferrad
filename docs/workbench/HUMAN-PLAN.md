# Next field / human calibration plan

Before the first actual loosening, use two roles: experienced installer and capture/log operator. Date and time of disassembly must be confirmed in the field; no schedule assumed from the historic summary.

1. With 2–3 target participants (including an experienced installer), ask each to find a named Kumpf/Arm/Krümmling, start GAP-04 or GAP-02, explain which value is historic, enter one measurement + photo, attach a verbatim explanation, and reset to Landseite. Record completion, elapsed time, errors and assistance. Aim ≤15 s finding; automation timing is not human evidence.
2. Repeat on a physical current iPhone/Safari and at 320 px equivalent, in daylight and at 200 % text size. Check touch with realistic hands and interruptions. Do not assume emulation covers camera chooser, wet hands, low memory or storage eviction.
3. Confirm LAND/WATER and origin (HUMAN-01), real physical IDs and slot mapping. Mark the fixed datum and three independent scale/control distances (GAP-01). Save and export before leaving the site.
4. At the first opening, photograph partner surfaces, measure mortise/keil geometry and log the event (GAP-02/08). Never infer a compulsory assembly order merely by reversing disassembly.
5. Inspect Kumpf variants 24/22 mm and ring lengths without forcing A/B if neither fits. Keep source quote and interpretation separate (HUMAN-02/03, GAP-04).
6. Capture warning signs, moisture conditions, wedge fit, flow/impact history, wear and repair practice with named person/role and alternate accounts (HUMAN-05). Risk prompts are questions, not validated engineering advice.
7. Review exports with source photos, reject simulated records from field evidence, and promote claims only through a documented human review. Baseline conflicts remain until supported adjudication.

Release blockers for a field-ready claim: documented device test, demonstrated safe export/reimport of that device's photos, and successful no-help task completion by actual users. Geometry/registration remains hypothesis-only until independent current measurements exist.
